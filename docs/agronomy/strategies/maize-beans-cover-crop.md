# Maize followed by black beans and a cover crop

AI-assisted draft: OpenAI Codex (gpt-6.1-sol). Local expert review pending.

Sequence: maize, black beans, then a cover crop of an unspecified species or mixture.
Engine candidate: `maize_beans_cover_crop`. The steps have no assigned calendar.

## Why

This option combines the bean and cover entries in a third comparison sequence.
The engine averages their provisional traits and applies its named rules.

## Evidence

- [FAO conservation agriculture principles](https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture/overview/conservation-agriculture-principles/en)
  supports diversification; three entries do not establish three species when one
  is unspecified or a mixture repeats a species.
- [CEDA historical evidence](../../data/ceda-evidence.md) provides maize and bean
  literature references, not validation of this three-step sequence.

## Possible benefits

Allows comparison of an additional cover period alongside the harvested crops.
Benefits shown numerically are model assumptions rather than measured field gains.

## Gaps

Cover species, bean variety, period lengths, water supply, labour and economic
information are missing. Three steps do not mean three harvests per year. The
engine does not weight scores by duration or calculate annual profit. Its range
is a heuristic, not a probability of success.

## Risks and local review

A local expert must check the seasonal calendar, cover establishment and
termination, crop compatibility and the farmer's resources. The generic cover
entry's assumed nitrogen benefit must not be taken as a fertilizer prescription.
