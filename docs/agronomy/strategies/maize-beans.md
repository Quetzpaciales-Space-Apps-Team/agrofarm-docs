# Maize followed by black beans

AI-assisted draft: OpenAI Codex (gpt-6.1-sol). Local expert review pending.

Sequence: maize, then black beans. Engine candidate: `maize_beans`.
Successive periods do not imply two feasible harvests in one calendar year.

## Why

This candidate compares a cereal and legume sequence with the maize/fallow example.
The engine adds provisional nitrogen and soil-structure bonuses.

## Evidence

- [FAO conservation agriculture principles](https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture/overview/conservation-agriculture-principles/en)
  supports species diversification as a principle; it does not establish the engine's bonuses.
- [CEDA historical evidence](../../data/ceda-evidence.md) contains maize and
  common-bean trial references. These do not verify current black-bean performance.

## Possible benefits

Lets the farmer compare a sequence with another harvested crop. Any nitrogen,
income or market benefit shown by the engine depends on unvalidated assumptions.

## Gaps

Bean variety, nodulation, residue handling, fertility, prices, costs and planting
windows are unknown. The engine treats the sequence as repeating and applies one
flat bonus. It does not calculate nitrogen in kg/ha or financial returns.
Score ranges are provisional, not measured probabilities.

## Risks and local review

Check water availability, pest history, seed availability and the actual market
with a local expert. Do not reduce fertilizer on the strength of a score. Confirm
that the farmer's bean type matches the black-bean entry before using it locally.
